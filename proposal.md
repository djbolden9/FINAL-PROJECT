## In just a sentence or two, summarize your project. (E.g., "A website that lets you buy and sell stocks.")

A Pokémon team builder game which given an opponent team, determines how well your team fares against the other. 

## In a paragraph or more, detail your project. What will your software do? What features will it have? How will it be executed?

There will be numerous lists of possible Pokémon, set opponent teams, and a random team generator. The user will be prompted to create their team when shown the opponents, though they have a value limit. Depending on a Pokémon's base stat they will contain a value ranging from around 5-20, the team must be within a certain limit (currently thinking 100). There is no battle system, rather a battle matrix simulation, that is how the winner will be determined. The user will chose the moves of their Pokémon, there will be a hard mode where the user's Pokémon cannot have held items. The game will be created in PyGame.
